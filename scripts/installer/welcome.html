Install UnZip

UnZip is a free macOS file explorer. It also opens archives, plays music and video, connects to FTP / FTPS / SFTP, and shares files on your local network.

This installer copies UnZip to Applications and registers the Finder service Zip with UnZip.

There is no license agreement and no account. Anyone may install and use it.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
